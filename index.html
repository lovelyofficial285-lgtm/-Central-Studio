About Nails Bar @ Central Studio

Welcome to Nails Bar @ Central Studio

Nails Bar @ Central Studio in Finchley is a modern nail salon offering stylish, high-quality nail care in a relaxed and friendly setting. We specialise in flawless acrylic nail extensions, detailed manicures and pampering pedicures.

Whether you want BIAB nails, gel polish, a fresh set of acrylics or expert care for natural nails, our skilled technicians provide a personalised approach to help you leave feeling polished, confident and beautifully finished.
